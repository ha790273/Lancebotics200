Who We Are

Welcome to LanceBotics

LanceBotics is the robotics team of Leaside High School, situated in the Borough of East York in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. We compete in the VEX Robotics Competition, building and programming robots to compete on the world stage.

  • Competing at regional, provincial, and international VEX events
  • Promoting STEM education in the Leaside community
  • Providing real-world engineering and programming experience
  • 5+ consecutive years at the VEX World Championship
