History of Lancebotics

Lancebotics was founded by a few students in 2013. Matthew Gehring, Zach Bolté, and Spencer Ploeger, alongside a Staff Advisor and Mentor Mr. Vincent Lu. Between 2013 - 2020, we had a total of 6 subteams. As of 2023 to the present day,we have 3 new subteams of 200G, 200K and Media.
