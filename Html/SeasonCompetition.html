Current Season

Override 2026-2027 Season

This year, the VEX Robotics Competition game is called "Override". The game is played on a 12ft x 12ft field with two alliances of two teams each. The objective of the game is to score points by placing cubes in goals and by controlling the field elements. The team with the most points at the end of the match wins.
