Sponsorship

Support LanceBotics

LanceBotics is Leaside High Schools VEX Robotics team. Sponsorship helps us pay for parts, registration fees, tools, travel, and the materials we need to build competitive robots.

Where support goes

Robotics is expensive. A single season can include new motors, structural parts, sensors, batteries, competition registration, replacement tools, and travel costs. Sponsorship makes it possible for more students to participate and for the team to keep improving throughout the season.

Support can be financial, or it can come through donated tools, parts, materials, mentorship, printing, transportation help, or workshop supplies.

Common needs

  • 02

  • 03

  • 04

    Mentorship

    Engineering, programming, CAD, business, media, and outreach support.
